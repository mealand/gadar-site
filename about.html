Our story

Our Story

Every event is a remarkably memorable experience

Gadar Events began in Kaduna with a simple belief: people remember how an occasion made them feel. The welcome at the door, the warmth of the hosts, the flow of the evening and the care in every small detail.

What started as a passion for beautiful celebrations has grown into a complete events house. Today our planners, hosts, chefs and the Gadar Ushering team deliver weddings, state functions and live shows in every region of the country.

Whether you are hosting fifty guests or five thousand, you receive the same promise. We listen to you, we design around you, and we deliver an experience that feels entirely your own.
